Important Safety Disclaimer

Inherent Risks of Gas Blending

Breathing incorrect gas mixtures while diving can result in serious injury or death, including but not limited to:

  • Oxygen toxicity (CNS or pulmonary)
  • Hypoxia (insufficient oxygen)
  • Nitrogen narcosis
  • Decompression sickness
  • Hypercapnia (CO₂ retention)

ALWAYS ANALYZE YOUR FINAL MIX WITH A CALIBRATED OXYGEN AND HELIUM ANALYZER BEFORE EVERY DIVE.
